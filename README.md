# Photo 2048

2048 where the tiles are your own photos.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser, or host the folder anywhere static (for GitHub Pages: Settings → Pages → deploy from this branch, root folder).

Swipe on the board on a phone, or use the arrow keys on a computer.

## Photos

Under the board there's a square for each tile value, from 2 up to 2048.

- **Upload photos** fills the empty squares in order. If every square already has a photo, it replaces them starting from 2.
- Tap a square to pick the photo for that tile.
- Tiles without a photo show their number.
- Tap **Hide** next to the Photos heading to collapse the squares, and **Show** to bring them back. The choice is remembered.

Photos are cropped to a square, shrunk, and saved in your browser on your device. Nothing is uploaded to a server.
